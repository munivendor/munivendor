Definitions

Agency

This is the government entity issuing the solicitation.

Agency MuniVendor User

These are team members from the government agency who actively utilize the MuniVendor platform to manage all the opportunities originating from the government agency. This almost always includes the Qualified Purchasing Agent and other members of the Purchasing Department. In some cases, it may also include the Business Administrator, and/or other team members who are actively involved in writing and preparing the opportunity before publication.

Authorizing Official

This is the person, employed by the Offeror’s company, who has the authority to submit offers on behalf of the Offeror’s company. Please note that the version of MuniVendor currently in use only permits one authorizing official. Multiple authorizing officials for offerors is a feature that MuniVendor plans to launch in February 2026.

Contractor

Upon executing a contract, a successful offeror becomes a contractor.

Decision Makers

These are team members from the government agency who make decisions pertaining to offers (submitted responses) from offerors (prospective vendors). Such decision makers may include, for example, the Mayor, Business Administrator, Councilmembers, Department Heads, and other personnel assigned to a decision making committee. Decision Makers do not ever log into the MuniVendor platform. Instead, they receive an email with the full contents of the offer (submission package) for which they are decision makers when the agency’s QPA opens the offers.

Offer

This is the completed submission of an offeror that contains all the content, forms, documents and certifications required in the agency’s solicitation specifications. This term replaces words such as “bids”, “responses”, and “proposals”.

Offeror

The entity submitting a response to the solicitation. This word replaces the words organization, company, bidders, proposers, vendors, contactors, and respondents.

Offeror MuniVendor User

These are team members from the offeror’s company who actively utilize the MuniVendor platform to create, manage and submit offers to the government agency. Please note that the version of MuniVendor currently in use only permits one user. Multiple users/logins for offerors is a feature that MuniVendor plans to launch in February 2026.

Solicitation

This is the formal publication by which an agency invites offerors to respond with an offer to provide goods and services to the agency. This term replaces phrases such as “request for bids”, “request for offers”, “request for proposals”, etc.
