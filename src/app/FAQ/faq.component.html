MuniVendor FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Files - Access & Control

Uh oh. I submitted my offer, but now I cannot remember if I uploaded the correct documents. Is there any way that a MuniVendor team member can check to verify what I uploaded?

Neither the MuniVendor technical support, nor its software development team have the ability to manually retrieve documents from the system. Every offeror upload is the intellectual property of the offeror prior to submission, and once it is submitted, it becomes the property of the government agency. All files uploaded to the MuniVendor system are encrypted. Only when the government agency opens the solicitation submissions do the files become decrypted and readable to the government agency.

Uh oh. I submitted my offer, but now I cannot remember if I uploaded the correct documents. Is there any way that a MuniVendor team member can set my offer status from Submitted to In Progress?

New Jersey public procurement law currently prohibits this. Once an offeror submits their offer through the MuniVendor eProcurement platform, the same public procurement laws apply to your sealed submission packet. Your digital submission through MuniVendor must mimic the same behavior as though you had submitted the offer manually, either by mailing it, or by dropping it off at the government agency. Just as the government agency will not return your submission packet to you, and just as the post office will not return your mailed submission packet to you after you mail it out, MuniVendor cannot "un-submit" your submission.

I have used other eProcurement platforms before, and they allow me to roll back my offer status from Submitted to In Progress up until the submission due date. Why do they allow this functionality, but MuniVendor does not?

MuniVendor was built to streamline and digitize the government procurement process in the State of New Jersey. As such, Munivendor was developed to be consistent with New Jersey public procurement law. Plainly put, based on the legal opinion we obtained from our legal team when developing MuniVendor, any eProcurement platform that enables an offeror to submit an offer, and then roll-back its status to make the submission editable is not in compliance with New Jersey public procurement law. It is likely that government agencies utilizing an eProcurement platform that does enable this functionality do not know that the eProcurement platform in use is causing the government agency to be out of compliance with New Jersey public procurement law.

Will MuniVendor ever allow an offeror to roll-back their submission status from Submitted to In Progress?

MuniVendor's legal team is in regular communication with officials responsible for developing and publishing public procurement law in New Jersey. As public procurement laws in New Jersey evolve, MuniVendor functionality offered to both offerors and government agencies will also evolve.

Can a MuniVendor team member log into my MuniVendor account on my behalf?

Neither the MuniVendor technical support, nor its software development team have the ability to log into or access MuniVendor accounts, regardless of whether or not they belong to the offeror or to the government agency. No MuniVendor team member will ever ask you for your password, nor will they ever offer to access your MuniVendor account on your behalf.

Can I email documents to the MuniVendor team to upload for me to my MuniVendor account?

Neither the MuniVendor technical support, nor its software development team have the ability to log into or access MuniVendor accounts, regardless of whether or not they belong to the offeror or to the government agency. No MuniVendor team member will ever ask you for your password, nor will they ever offer to access your MuniVendor account on your behalf.

What happens to my files when I upload them? Who has access to my files?

Neither the MuniVendor technical support, nor its software development team have the ability to manually retrieve documents from the system. Every offeror upload is the intellectual property of the offeror company prior to submission, and once it is submitted to the government agency, it becomes the property of the government agency. All files uploaded to the MuniVendor system are encrypted; this is the digital equivalent of a "sealed submission packet". Only when the government agency opens the solicitation submissions do the files become decrypted and readable to the government agency.

Are my files accessible to MuniVendor personnel after the government agency has opened the solicitations?

Neither the MuniVendor technical support, nor its software development team have the ability to access, view, edit, delete or upload documents from any offeror or government agency account. This is always the case, 100% of the time, regardless of the current status of the solicitation.

Costs

What does it cost to register for MuniVendor?

There is no cost to offerors to register for MuniVendor. This means that it does not cost offerors any money to view solicitations from the government agencies that use the MuniVendor eProcurement platform.

What does it cost to submit an offer to a government agency?

There is no cost to offerors to respond to government agency solicitations in 2025.

In 2026, offerors will be charged $49 per offer submission in response to government agency solicitations. Offerors will have the option of purchasing "submission packs" at a discount, prior to each submission. We developed this cost model after consulting with dozens of offerors in the State of New Jersey to determine the average cost that offerors incur when they respond manually to solicitations, which averages to approximately $80 per submission (paper, toner, shipping/manual delivery, payroll, etc.)

Over time, to save costs to offerors, as additional government agencies enroll in the MuniVendor platform, MuniVendor may evolve its pricing plan to a monthly subscription model.

What does it cost to view a government agency's solicitation details?

There is no cost to offerors to view a government agency solicitation. The only charge to offerors is incurred when they submit their offer.

I am on the Munivendor platform, but when I go to view the solicitation details, my only option is to "respond". Does this mean I can only view the solicitation details after paying to submit?

There is no cost to offerors to view a government agency solicitation. The only charge to offerors is incurred when they submit their offer. To view solicitation details, select "Respond". Then scroll down to the bottom of the page and name your offer/response, and make sure there is an authorized official selected underneath. When you click on the Next button and are in Step 2 - Details, you will be able to view and/or download the solicitation information. Step 3 - Documents is where you will download the government agency required forms, and then upload your completed, signed, scanned and/or notarized documents. Your offeror account will only be charged when you are Step 4 - Review and then click on the blue Submit button.
