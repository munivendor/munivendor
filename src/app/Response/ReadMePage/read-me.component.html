MuniVendor eProcurement Portal Guide

Introduction

Welcome to the MuniVendor eProcurement portal. MuniVendor was developed to address the inadequacies and inefficiencies that currently plague the public procurement process specific to the State of New Jersey. It was developed in collaboration with New Jersey public procurement subject matter experts from both the public and private sector. MuniVendor's goal is to streamline the public procurement process in New Jersey, digitizing a once manual, paper-driven process that reduces submission time from hours to minutes.

Definitions

MuniVendor uses terminology that is consistent with New Jersey public procurement, and also employs new terminology that they plan to use. To that end, there are some terms that offerors may not be familiar with. For example, the term "offeror" replaces the words organization, company, bidders, proposers, vendors, contactors, and respondents. For a full list of terms and their definitions, please click on the Definitions tab in the menu.

Maintenance & Enhancements

MuniVendor employs a full time software development team, all who live in the State of New Jersey. We continue to consult with offerors and NJ public procurement experts to develop additional ways to reduce the time and costs associated with responding to solicitations from NJ government agencies. If you have an idea for a feature that you would like to see in MuniVendor, please let us know by visiting https://munivendor.com/contact.

The Dashboard

On the top portion of the dashboard, offerors may filter the available solicitations by:

  • Solicitation name
  • Category name
  • Publication date
  • Solicitation ID
  • Close date
  • Solicitation status
  • Offeror status
  • Solicitation type

On the bottom part of the dashboard, offerors may view all the solicitations currently available through the government agencies using MuniVendor.

Dashboard Fields

Solicitation Name

This is the name of the solicitation.

Agency Organization Name

The name of the government agency that published the solicitation.

Solicitation ID

This ID is provided so that offerors such as yourself can easily reference the specific solicitation if/when you communicate with the public agency, or with MuniVendor's technical support team.

Type

The type of solicitation.

Category

When government agencies publish solicitations on MuniVendor, they select the category of the solicitation. There is always a category assigned to each solicitation, and they may add additional sub-categories to the solicitation.

Publish Date & Time

This is when the solicitation is available to offerors on MuniVendor.

Close Date & Time

This is the deadline that offerors have to submit their offer. MuniVendor will not allow offerors to respond to any solicitation that has passed the close date and time, even if it is by one (1) second.

Important: If a solicitation's close date and time is December 10, 2025 at 11:00 AM, and the actual date and time is December 10, 2025 and it is 11:00 AM, and one second has passed, the ability to submit the solicitation will have become disabled.

Offeror Status

This is the status of your offer (response) to the government agency's solicitation:

  • None - The offeror has not begun an offer.
  • In Progress - The offeror has begun an offer, but has not yet submitted the offer.
  • Submitted - The offeror has submitted the offer in response to the government agency's solicitation, and the government agency has received it. Remember, the government agency does not have the ability to view or download your offer until the status of the solicitation has changed from "Closed" to "Open".

Agency Status

This is the status of the solicitation itself:

  • Live - Offerors may respond to the solicitation.
  • Closed - The close date for the solicitation is now in the past, and offerors may no longer respond to this solicitation.
  • Open - The purchasing department of the government agency has changed the status from Closed to Open. This is a manual process (they only have to click a few buttons) that is performed during the opening, which generally happens shortly after the close date and time. Some government agencies will enable offerors to view the opening process in person, or virtually during a live screen-share. During this time, the government agency will publicly show which offerors responded to which solicitations.
  • Cancelled - The government agency cancelled the solicitation before the close date and time was reached.
Note: It is important to note that just because a government agency has received and opened your solicitation, this does not mean that the offeror has been qualified, nor does this mean that the offeror will obtain a contract from their offer.

Actions

In this section, offerors can begin, edit and submit their offers:

  • None - This means that no actions are available to the offeror. The status of None applies to solicitations that have either passed the close date and time, or have been cancelled by the government agency.
  • Select - This means that there are actions available to the offeror.
    • Respond - Offerors should select this to begin their offer (response).
    • Continue - Offerors who have begun an offer will see this option so that they can continue their offer if they did not already submit their offer to the government agency.
    • Delete - Offerors may select this option when they began an offer, but then decide that they do not plan to submit an offer in response to the solicitation.

Offeror Profile

Please make sure to fill out your offeror profile on the Offeror Profile page. This data is shared with the government agencies to which you submit your offers. Over time, MuniVendor will enable you to enter additional content.

Authorizing Official

This is the person, employed by the offeror's company, who has the authority to submit offers on behalf of the Offeror's company. When you create an offer to a government agency solicitation, you must select an Authorizing Official from a dropdown. That dropdown pulls its content from the Authorizing Official section in the Offeror Profile page, so make sure to fill it out before you begin your offer.

Creating, Editing & Submitting Offers

Offerors begin the offer response process by clicking on the Select button in the Actions row of the corresponding solicitation, and selecting Respond. If you have already begun an offer, and then come back to the dashboard to continue your offer, select Continue to continue your offer.

Note: The following is an explainer video we developed showing offerors how to respond to a government agency solicitation using MuniVendor. Clicking on this link will take you to that YouTube video. https://munivendor.com/offeror-response.

Step 1 - Basics

Agency Section

This section displays content entered by the government agency responsible for this solicitation. The content is intentionally greyed out, as there is nothing for the offeror to enter in this portion.

Offeror Section

Here, you will enter the title/name of your offer, and select the Authorizing Official from the dropdown.

After you enter the above information, click on the blue Next button, located toward the bottom right of the page.

Step 2 - Details

The details page contains the content for the specific solicitation. Offerors can view the content within the MuniVendor platform, or can click on the blue Download as PDF button, located toward the top left of the screen.

The blue Back and Next buttons are both located on the top right and bottom right of this page. Click Back to make changes in the Basics page, or click Next to move on to the next step.

Step 3 - Documents

This is the step in which you, the offeror, will upload your offer and all the required documents and forms for this solicitation. You will not be able to submit your offer until you have uploaded all the required content.

Agency Required Forms & Documents

Form Name - The name of each form.

File Actions:

  • Download Icon - The download icon-button will download the original form from the government agency publishing the solicitation.
  • Upload Icon - The upload icon-button will enable you to upload the completed content.
Note: It is important to note that once you have uploaded the completed document, if you click on the download button, you will be downloading a copy of the file you uploaded, and not the original form supplied by Munivendor.

All MuniVendor participating government agencies use the New Jersey State issued version of these forms.

For cybersecurity reasons, you must upload your content either as a flat PDF, or jpg files.

Status:

  • Incomplete - This means that you have not yet uploaded the completed form.
  • Complete - This means that you have successfully uploaded your form.
Important: MuniVendor does not currently scan your documents or ensure that you have uploaded the correct document, or that the document is complete. You, the offeror, bear the responsibility to ensure that you properly complete and upload your document. Again, you, the offeror, must make certain that you completed the forms as per the government agency's instructions.

Agency Required Forms & Documents (Notarization Required)

These forms must be notarized, scanned and then uploaded to MuniVendor here.

Form Name - The name of each form.

File Actions:

  • Download Icon - The download icon-button will download the original form from the government agency publishing the solicitation.
  • Upload Icon - The upload icon-button will enable you to upload the completed content.
Note: It is important to note that once you have uploaded the completed document, if you click on the download button, you will be downloading a copy of the file you uploaded, and not the original form supplied by Munivendor.

All MuniVendor participating government agencies use the New Jersey State issued version of these forms.

For cybersecurity reasons, you must upload your content either as a flat PDF, or jpg files.

Offeror Uploads

Click on the blue Add button to upload your proposal, brochures, and other offer-specific documents that are relevant to your solicitation. This may include, but is not necessarily limited to the following. You may upload each document separately, or you can combine these files and upload them in one PDF.

  • Business Registration Certificate
  • W-9
  • Insurance Forms

The blue Back and Next buttons are both located on the top right and bottom right of this page. Click Next to move on to the next step.

Step 4 - Review

The Review page is the final step before offer submission. Here, you can review all the solicitation response content, such as the Offeror Response Title, Authorizing Official, and all the documents you uploaded. Make sure to double check all the content you've uploaded to confirm that you have no errors or omissions.

Once you are satisfied with the content, click on the blue Submit button to complete your offer submission.

Important: Once you click on the Submit button, you will no longer be able to edit or delete your offer, just as if you had manually dropped it off or mailed it to the government agency that published the solicitation.

Costs

Registration is Free

There is no cost to offerors to register for MuniVendor. This means that it does not cost offerors any money to view solicitations from the government agencies that use the MuniVendor eProcurement platform.

Submission Costs

There is no cost to offerors to respond to government agency solicitations in 2025.

In 2026, offerors will be charged $49 per offer submission in response to government agency solicitations. Offerors will have the option of purchasing "submission packs" at a discount, prior to each submission. We developed this cost model after consulting with dozens of offerors in the State of New Jersey to determine the average cost that offerors incur when they respond manually to solicitations, which averaged to approximately $80 per submission (paper, toner, shipping/manual delivery, payroll, etc.)

Over time, to save costs to offerors, as additional government agencies enroll in the MuniVendor platform, MuniVendor may evolve its pricing plan to a monthly subscription model.
